# Instructor notes for the PDF-based course

The active course follows the supplied 36-page student manual. Its seven modules, 82 core lesson topics, 81 practice prompts and 16 sample recipes are organized into 127 classroom slides. Source page numbers appear on each slide.

- **Page 3:** this is an internal Cafe de La Rue course, not an official TESDA competency assessment or government-issued qualification.
- **Page 4:** the contents list mentions worksheets, daily records, a final written examination and a completion record. These appendices are not present in this supplied 36-page file. They have not been invented or added to the course.
- **Page 9:** contains only the running header and page number; no instructional lesson was extracted from it.
- **Pages 13–15:** 18 g dry coffee, 36 g beverage yield, a 1:2 ratio and approximately 25–30 seconds are training starting guidelines. Yield is mass, not volume or added water.
- **Pages 16–17:** retain the manual's milk guidance, including its approximate 55–65°C dairy serving target and equipment-specific handling.
- **Pages 18–34:** the 16 recipes are illustrative training formulas, not proprietary Cafe de La Rue recipes. Validate ingredients, sweetness, dilution, ice displacement and cup capacity before standardizing.
- **Pages 35–36:** demonstrate operations, hygiene and sanitation with the café's documented procedures, supplier directions and equipment instructions.
- Existing photographs and illustrations remain in their original colors as classroom illustrations. They do not replace the manual's text or supply missing iced-drink photos.
- The downloadable PDF is an unchanged copy of the user's supplied file.
